``` r
data("penguins", package = "palmerpenguins")
```

The `penguins` dataset contains information about penguins, including
their species (Adelie, Gentoo, Chinstrap); island (Torgersen, Biscoe,
Dream); bill length; bill depth; flipper length; body mass; sex (male,
female, NA); and year of observation (2007, 2008, 2009).

The size of the dataset is 344 observations and 8 variables. The mean
flipper length is 200.92 mm.
