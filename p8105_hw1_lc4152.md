The mean works for `sample_random` and `vec_positive`, which are numeric
and logical variables. It doesn’t work for `vec_char` and `vec_factor`.

Apply `as.numeric` to logical, char, and factor.

``` r
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, vec_positive))
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, vec_char))
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, vec_factor))
```

Converting the logical variable to numeric changes `TRUE` to 1 and
`FALSE` to 0. This explains why taking the mean of a logical variable
works.

For character, it produces `NA` values because the character strings
cannot be interpreted as numbers.

For factor variable, it returns the integer codes corresponding to the
factor levels.

Therefore, these numbers represent the internal coding of the factor
levels and do not necessarily have a meaningful numerical
interpretation.
